02 · Scientific Roadmap

Four Interlocking Research Pillars

Across its three-year program, LLADIGA investigates interactive learning from external behavioral dynamics down to internal model mechanisms.

Pillar I · Interactive Learning

Multi-Turn Post-Training & Reinforcement Learning

Developing training regimes—spanning imitation on successful interaction traces, preference optimization, and online multi-turn reinforcement learning—that harness programmatic Game Master rewards and intrinsic communicative feedback rather than static human annotations.

Multi-Turn RL lm-playpen Intrinsic Feedback
Pillar II · Generalization

Cross-Game Skill Transfer & Curricula

Investigating how functional skills acquired in one family of dialogue games—such as spatial mental modeling, hypothesis pruning, or pragmatic speaker-listener coordination—transfer to unseen games, roles, and broader reasoning tasks, informing principled training curricula.

Skill Generalization Curriculum Learning Role Asymmetry
Pillar III · Interpretability

Representations & Internal Model Dynamics

Looking inside fine-tuned and RL-adapted compact models to characterize what interactive learning modifies in parameter and activation space—disentangling surface format compliance from genuine strategic and pragmatic mechanisms.

Mechanistic Probing Parameter Dynamics Transparent AI
Pillar IV · Benchmarking & Sustainability

Functional Assessment with Compact Open Models

Advancing reference-free, interactive evaluation instruments that measure whether small, open-weights language models can match or exceed far larger architectures on purposeful communication—reducing both data hunger and environmental cost.

clembench Small Open LLMs Green AI
03 · Priority Research Network

Embedded in DFG Priority Programme SPP 2556 “LaSTing”

LLADIGA is an integral project of the German Research Foundation (DFG) Priority Programme SPP 2556 — LaSTing (“Robust Assessment & Safe Applicability of Language Modeling: Foundations for a New Field of Language Science & Technology”), coordinated across leading computational linguistics and AI groups in Europe.

Within LaSTing and the bilateral DFG – Autonomous Province of Bolzano (D-SUD LAV) partnership, LLADIGA bridges foundational questions in human-inspired language science with rigorous engineering for trustworthy, resource-conscious language technology.

Visit Priority Area “LaSTing” ↗ DFG – South Tyrol Programme (D-SUD LAV) ↗

LaSTing Core Focus Areas Addressed

  • ✓ Behavioral Assessment: Dynamic, multi-turn dialogue probes beyond static benchmarks
  • ✓ Representations & Mechanisms: Tracking structural changes inside adapted models
  • ✓ Training & Optimization: Learning from interactive rewards and communicative success
  • ✓ Resource Efficiency: Empowering compact open-weights models with less data
  • ✓ Alternative Paradigms: Purpose-driven, developmentally inspired acquisition

LLADIGA — Learning Language with Dialogue Games

A 3-year bilateral research project carried out by the University of Potsdam in collaboration with the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano within the bilateral collaboration framework between the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) — Priority Programme SPP 2556 “LaSTing” — and the Autonomous Province of Bolzano – South Tyrol (Joint Projects D-SUD LAV).
