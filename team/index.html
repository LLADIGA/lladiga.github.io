Bilateral Partnership

Host Universities

Carried out within the joint Germany – South Tyrol bilateral research framework (DFG & Autonomous Province of Bolzano, D-SUD LAV).

Germany · Potsdam

University of Potsdam

Chair of Foundations of Computational Linguistics (colabPotsdam), Department of Linguistics—pioneering situated dialogue modeling, interactive language grounding, and game-based LLM evaluation (clembench / lm-playpen).

University of Potsdam ↗
Italy · South Tyrol

Free University of Bozen-Bolzano

Faculty of Engineering—advancing natural language processing, multimodal and pragmatic reasoning, model interpretability, and data-efficient language learning.

Free University of Bozen-Bolzano ↗

LLADIGA — Learning Language with Dialogue Games

A 3-year bilateral research project carried out by the University of Potsdam in collaboration with the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano within the bilateral collaboration framework between the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) — Priority Programme SPP 2556 “LaSTing” — and the Autonomous Province of Bolzano – South Tyrol (Joint Projects D-SUD LAV).

Realizzata con il contributo della Provincia autonoma di Bolzano – Alto Adige.
Mit Unterstützung der Autonomen Provinz Bozen – Südtirol und der Deutschen Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG).
