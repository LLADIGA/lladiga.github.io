The LM Playschool: Improving Language Models through Learning from Dialogue Interaction

Organized by the LLADIGA project team and collaborators at EMNLP 2026 in Budapest, The LM Playschool introduced a community-wide shared task built on the lm-playpen workbench. Participating teams explored supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning over dialogue game interactions to improve interactive competence (clemscore) on held-out games without sacrificing general capabilities (statscore).

Open Benchmarks & Academic Exchange

Accessible Shared Tasks & Reproducible Models

Through The LM Playschool, open evaluation suites (clembench), and publicly accessible compact specialist checkpoints on Hugging Face, LLADIGA lowers the barrier for universities and independent labs to study interactive post-training without hyperscale compute.

LLADIGA — Learning Language with Dialogue Games

A 3-year bilateral research project carried out by the University of Potsdam in collaboration with the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano within the bilateral collaboration framework between the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) — Priority Programme SPP 2556 “LaSTing” — and the Autonomous Province of Bolzano – South Tyrol (Joint Projects D-SUD LAV).
