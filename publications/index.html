LLADIGA — Learning Language with Dialogue Games

A 3-year bilateral research project carried out by the University of Potsdam in collaboration with the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano within the bilateral collaboration framework between the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) — Priority Programme SPP 2556 “LaSTing” — and the Autonomous Province of Bolzano – South Tyrol (Joint Projects D-SUD LAV).

Realizzata con il contributo della Provincia autonoma di Bolzano – Alto Adige.
Mit Unterstützung der Autonomen Provinz Bozen – Südtirol und der Deutschen Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG).
