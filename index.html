01 · Project Vision

From Passive Text Prediction to Purposeful Communication

Bridging cognitive inspirations from child language acquisition with principled multi-turn learning and evaluation for language models.

Large Language Models (LLMs) have learned language through incremental, data-intensive phases: first by learning to predict the next word in massive text corpora, next by learning to follow instructions, and finally by aligning outputs to human preferences. This is Artificial Intelligence nowadays—powerful, yet extraordinarily data-hungry.

Human children are dramatically more sample-efficient: they need vastly less linguistic input to become fluent communicators. Why? Because children do not passively absorb static archives of text; they learn to use language for a purpose—to coordinate, ask questions, resolve misunderstandings, and interact with their caregivers and the physical and social world they inhabit.

LLADIGA (Learning Language with Dialogue Games) addresses this foundational question directly. By turning structured, goal-oriented, multi-turn dialogue games into active learning arenas, we investigate how language models can acquire communicative and reasoning capabilities from interaction signals rather than sheer corpus scale—making AI training not only more transparent and grounded, but also substantially less data-hungry and less ecologically damaging.

LLADIGA is a 3-year bilateral project carried out jointly by the University of Potsdam (led by Prof. Dr. David Schlangen) and the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano (unibz) (led by Prof. Raffaella Bernardi) within the bilateral collaboration framework between the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) and the Autonomous Province of Bolzano – South Tyrol (Joint Projects D-SUD LAV).

🎯 Language as Goal-Directed Action

In dialogue games, language simultaneously defines the task constraints (rules) and serves as the medium of action—requiring perspective-taking, pragmatic adaptation, and multi-turn planning.

🌿 Sample & Ecological Efficiency

Replacing brute-force corpus scaling with rich interactive feedback targets stronger generalization in compact, open-weights language models with a fraction of the energy footprint.

🔬 Behavioral & Internal Probing

Controlled game environments disentangle rule-following from strategic competence externally while providing clean diagnostic lenses into internal model representations and parameter updates.

03 · Scientific Agenda

Four Interlocking Research Pillars

Across its three-year program, LLADIGA investigates interactive learning from external behavioral dynamics down to internal model mechanisms.

Pillar I · Interactive Learning

Multi-Turn Post-Training & Reinforcement Learning

Developing training regimes—spanning imitation on successful interaction traces, preference optimization, and online multi-turn reinforcement learning—that harness programmatic Game Master rewards and intrinsic communicative feedback rather than static human annotations.

Multi-Turn RL lm-playpen Intrinsic Feedback
Pillar II · Generalization

Cross-Game Skill Transfer & Curricula

Investigating how functional skills acquired in one family of dialogue games—such as spatial mental modeling, hypothesis pruning, or pragmatic speaker-listener coordination—transfer to unseen games, roles, and broader reasoning tasks, informing principled training curricula.

Skill Generalization Curriculum Learning Role Asymmetry
Pillar III · Interpretability

Representations & Internal Model Dynamics

Looking inside fine-tuned and RL-adapted compact models to characterize what interactive learning modifies in parameter and activation space—disentangling surface format compliance from genuine strategic and pragmatic mechanisms.

Mechanistic Probing Parameter Dynamics Transparent AI
Pillar IV · Benchmarking & Sustainability

Functional Assessment with Compact Open Models

Advancing reference-free, interactive evaluation instruments that measure whether small, open-weights language models can match or exceed far larger architectures on purposeful communication—reducing both data hunger and environmental cost.

clembench Small Open LLMs Green AI
04 · Research Network & Framework

Embedded in DFG Priority Programme SPP 2556 “LaSTing”

LLADIGA is an integral project of the German Research Foundation (DFG) Priority Programme SPP 2556 — LaSTing (“Robust Assessment & Safe Applicability of Language Modeling: Foundations for a New Field of Language Science & Technology”), coordinated across leading computational linguistics and AI groups in Europe.

Within LaSTing and the bilateral DFG – Autonomous Province of Bolzano (D-SUD LAV) partnership, LLADIGA bridges foundational questions in human-inspired language science with rigorous engineering for trustworthy, resource-conscious language technology.

Visit Priority Area “LaSTing” (lasting-spp.org) ↗

LaSTing Core Focus Areas Addressed

  • ✓ Behavioral Assessment: Dynamic, multi-turn dialogue probes beyond static benchmarks
  • ✓ Representations & Mechanisms: Tracking structural changes inside adapted models
  • ✓ Training & Optimization: Learning from interactive rewards and communicative success
  • ✓ Resource Efficiency: Empowering compact open-weights models with less data
  • ✓ Alternative Paradigms: Purpose-driven, developmentally inspired acquisition
05 · Community Challenges in Learning in Interaction

The LM Playschool Workshop & Challenge

Catalyzing a global research community around post-training and evaluating language models through situated, goal-oriented dialogue interaction.

EMNLP 2026 · Budapest, Hungary Workshop & Shared Task ACL Anthology Proceedings

The LM Playschool: Improving Language Models through Learning from Dialogue Interaction

Organized by the LLADIGA project team and collaborators at EMNLP 2026 in Budapest, The LM Playschool introduced a community-wide shared task built on the lm-playpen workbench. Participating teams explored supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning over dialogue game interactions to improve interactive competence (clemscore) on held-out games without sacrificing general capabilities (statscore).
